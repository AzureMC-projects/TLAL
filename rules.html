FAIR PLAY / CLEAR STANDARDS

RULES & CATEGORIES

A clear, consistent standard helps every runner trust the board. These are the current site rules; the community should review and agree the exact category definitions before treating rankings as official.

01

VIDEO PROOF

Provide a public video covering the run from start to finish. Moderators must be able to verify the timing and relevant gameplay.

02

HONEST TIMES

Submit your own attempt. Do not edit, splice, or misrepresent footage. Disclose glitches, exploits, resets, or unusual routing in the notes.

03

VERSION & CHAPTER

Choose the correct chapter and provide the game version. Runs may be compared separately if updates materially change routes or timing.

04

CATEGORY OBJECTIVES

Choose Any% or 100% accurately. The site currently records these labels, but detailed completion requirements should be agreed and published by the community.

05

MODERATION

Every new submission starts pending. Only approved runs appear in public rankings. Staff may reject invalid runs or take down a run if new evidence or a rule violation is found.

06

APPEALS & CONDUCT

Be respectful when asking for a review. Include evidence and a concise explanation. Staff actions should include a reason and are recorded in the moderation audit log.
